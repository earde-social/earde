# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Earde serves members and moderators of technical communities who need focused real-time discussion without losing valuable knowledge in an endless chat history.

## Product Purpose

Earde helps technical communities discuss topics live, promote valuable conversations into structured threads, and reliably find that knowledge later. Success means communities remain active in the moment while their best discussions stay organized, discoverable, and useful over time.

## Positioning

Earde turns live community conversation into durable, searchable knowledge without splitting the community across separate chat and forum tools.

## Brand Personality

Focused, durable, and technically literate. Earde should combine Linear's disciplined interaction design and information density, the durable and legible discussion structure of classic technical forums, and Slack's familiar live-community navigation without copying Slack's visual style.

## Anti-references

Earde must not resemble a noisy consumer social feed, a playful gamified chat app, or a generic rounded-card SaaS dashboard. Avoid engagement bait, decorative gradients, excessive motion, and interfaces that make valuable discussion feel transient.

## Design Principles

1. **Make the durable path natural.** Moving from live conversation to structured, lasting knowledge should feel like a continuation of discussion, not a separate workflow.
2. **Keep attention on the community.** Navigation and controls should be familiar, compact, and quiet enough to disappear behind the conversation.
3. **Signal technical trustworthiness.** Information architecture, interaction states, and language should feel precise, dependable, and legible under sustained use.
4. **Preserve usefulness without JavaScript.** Core content and workflows should remain clear and functional in server-rendered HTML; enhancement should never become a prerequisite.
5. **Prefer structure over engagement mechanics.** Optimize for comprehension, retrieval, and healthy participation rather than attention capture or activity for its own sake.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve full keyboard operation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, semantic server-rendered HTML, reduced-motion preferences, and meaning that never depends on color alone.
